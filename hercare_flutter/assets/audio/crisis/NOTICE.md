# M10 attention chimes

Original procedural sine-note compositions generated by
`hercare_ml/scripts/crisis_audio.py` using only Python's standard library.
No sampled recordings, third-party music, network service or runtime synthesizer.

`support_chime.wav`: eight seconds, mono 16 kHz 16-bit PCM. Explicit user start,
with a controller deadline of at most fifteen seconds including initialization.
`hercare_urgent.wav` is the separate 2.4-second native Android notification sound
in `android/app/src/main/res/raw/`. Neither signal bypasses device volume,
notification permissions, silent mode, Do Not Disturb or channel preferences.

An attention sound does not summon assistance, send a guardian notification,
verify a call, or establish safety. Do not delay direct emergency help for it.
